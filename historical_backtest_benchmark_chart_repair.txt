===== HISTORICAL BACKTEST BENCHMARK CHART REPAIR =====

Issue:
- Historical 2008 Backtest chart showed SPY near 0 and QQQ missing.

Cause:
- growth_champion_reconstructed_stress_daily.csv had blank spy_daily_return/qqq_daily_return columns.
- Initial dashboard fallback used one-day returns on sparse strategy dates, which undercounted buy-and-hold benchmark returns.

Fix:
- Dashboard now reads SPY/QQQ adjusted prices from yahoo_ohlcv_price_cache.
- Benchmarks are aligned to the exact historical backtest dates.
- Returns are computed between consecutive strategy observation dates.
- All cumulative series start at 0% on the first shared date.

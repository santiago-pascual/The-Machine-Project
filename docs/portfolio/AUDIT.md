## Governance and leakage

Confirmed source issues: median imputation on entire dataset before split (`src/ml_core_model.py:_split_xy`), independent calibration/test partition medians (`src/meta_model_walk_forward_validation.py:_feature_matrix`), row-based splits without label-end purging in these functions, test-based model selection (`_best_model_name`), full-window default Kalman noise, legacy DSR difference mislabeled. These are research-path findings; impact on frozen model not quantified. Corporate actions: canonical manager downloads with auto_adjust=False, but actual choice of close/adjusted close and split treatment across tapes is not certified. Point-in-time universe not confirmed; existing `point_in_time_liquidity_governance.csv` blocks on high survivorship risk. Data timing, label overlap, cost and benchmark consistency remain open.

Later `archive/full_anti_overfitting_validation.py`: pivots by date/strategy with mean aggregation, permits missing values across strategies, applies sqrt(252) to mixed-frequency inputs. `deflated_sharpe_exact.csv` reports 2.0445 as a Sharpe difference and 0.00136 approximate p-value for v3 2015 window; not a certified probability DSR. Effective count three is rounded correlation-rank estimate for 60 columns, not whole research history. CSCV recorded fold flags recomputed (70/252/924 partitions, 77.14/67.86/63.20% below median); no rerun of selection matrix. The later reality check uses IID bootstrap, fills missing excess with zero and defaults to zero benchmark; not a dependence-aware SPA. Reported p=0 is a finite simulation count, not exact zero probability. `anti_overfitting_governance.csv` classifies research-only and says multiple-testing survival false. Legacy heuristic PBO is distinct.

`archive/purged_oos_validation.py` creates 28 expanding folds and five purged kfold evaluations of precomputed returns; 30 calendar-day purge and embargo, no reoptimization. Locked 12-month report (51 rows) claims +75.85%, Sharpe 2.666; prior research access and untouched status not independently established. Overlapping start windows and CSCV partitions are dependent. Fallback loader does not filter overlapping windows; do not treat as safe if primary tape absent.
